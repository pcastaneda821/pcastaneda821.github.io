Overview

Eating out shouldn't feel like a risk.

For millions of people living with food allergies, dietary restrictions, or medical conditions like celiac disease or IBS, eating out at a restaurant isnt' just inconvenient, it can be very dangerous. Existing apps like Yelp or Google Maps show you restaurants, but give you almost no meaningful information about whether the food is actually safe for you to eat.

Better Belli is a mobile app designed specifically for this underserved audience. It combines personalized onboarding, an allergy-aware restaurant map, and a first-of-its-kind menu scanner that highlights ingredients in real time.

"How might we help people with dietary restrictions find and enjoy restaurants safely, without having to interrogate every server or guess at every ingredient?"

Research

The anxiety of eating out

We conducted user interviews and surveys with people who manage food allergies and dietary restrictions. The findings were consistent: eating out caused real anxiety, and existing tools fell far shoot of meeting their needs.

Existing apps don't help.

Yelp and Google Maps show restaurants but give no allergy or ingredient information.

Relying on servers is risky

Users couldn't always trust verbal assurances from restaurant staff about ingredients.

Menu are hard to parse

Reading through long menus to find safe options is exhausting and time-consuming.

One size doesn't fit all

Restrictions vary widely. Allerygies, religious, pregnancy, Gi conditions all need different handling.

Solution

Three features, one safe experience

Better Belli is built around three core features that work together to make Designingout stress-free for people with dietary needs.

Personalized Onboarding

A 4-step setup flow that collects allergies, dietary restrictions, GI conditions, pregnancy needs, and religious restrictions to fully customize the experience.

Allergy-Safe Map

A map-first home screen showing nearby restaurants filtered to match the user's profile. Search bt cuisine, distance, or dietary need.

Menu Scanner

Point your camera at any physical menu. Better Belli highlights items in real-time to: green meaning safe to eat, red meaning avoid based on your restrictions.

Outcomes

What we learned

Better Belli reinforced how powerful it is to design for a specific, underserved user group. When you deeply understand someone's pain the right design decisions become much clearer.

The menu scanner was out most innovative feature and also our biggest design challenge. Getting the red/green highlighting system to feel intuitive without being alarming required multiple rounds of iteration based on user feedback.
