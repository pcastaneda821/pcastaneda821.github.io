Overview

Spotify is great when it's just you and the music.

Spotify is the world's most popular streaming platform, but it's designed for browsing and discovery but not for focused, intentional listening. When you're trying to study or work, the home page is full of distraction: friend activity, new releases, trending content, and recommendations that pull you away from studying.

We identified an opportunity to add a Focus Mode to the desktop app which will provide a distraction-free environment that locks the interface to only what you need: your playlists and curated study/lo-fi content. Two supporting features round out the solution: a Do Not Disturb toggle in the nav bar to activate Focus Mode instantly, and a Genre Restriction setting to filter out genres users don't want to be played.

"How might we help Spotify users stay focused and in flow, without having to leave the app or fight against its interface?"

Research

What we heard from users

We conducted user interviews and surverys with Spotify users who regularly listen while studying or working. The core tension that emerged: people love having music on while they focus, but Spotify's interface actively competes for their attention.

Friend activity is distracting

Study and lo-fi playlists are buried under trending and algorithmic content.

No genre control

Usres had no way to prevent certain genres from appearing in their recommendations.

No "focus" state exists

Users wanted a single toggle to simplify the entire interface when it's time to work.

solution

Three feature, one focused experience

Rather than redesigning Spotify from scratch, we added three targeted features that work together to create a distraction-free listening environment.

Do Not Disturb Toggle

A moon icon added to the top nav bar. One click activates Focus Mode instantly, locking the interface and hiding all distraction.

Focus Mode

Collapses the sidebar, hides Friend Activity, and transforms the home screen to show only your playlists and curated Study and Lo-fi sections.

Genre Restriction

A new setting that lets users select genres to exclude from their recommendations to give users real control over their listening experience.

Outcomes

What we learned

This project reinforced how much context of use matters in product design. Spotify's interface is opitmized for discovery, but the same design works against users when their goal is focus, not exploration. The best solutions weren't about adding more, they were about intelligently removing what wasn't needed in the moment.

The Genre Restriction feaure also highlighted an important gap in Spotify's current settings: users want more control over what they hear, not just more recommendations.
